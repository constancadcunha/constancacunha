Room III — The Gallery

Where I've painted

01

Remote work on a live real estate platform used by 500+ people in Zurich. Redesigned the homepage, dashboards, and feed to make them actually usable — then worked directly with engineers to ship it across 2 release cycles.

Built a CSS module component library from atomic design principles. Implementation time for new features dropped by roughly ~30%. Features went through multiple rounds of stakeholder and user feedback before shipping — balancing what people asked for with what the product actually needed.

02

Two months in Lisbon. Built an internal STB device management system from nothing in under 2 weeks. Before it existed, onboarding a device took several days. After: <4 hours.

Also automated repetitive workflows and built structured data exports used across all operational teams. Most of it came from talking to the people who'd actually use the thing before writing a single line of code.

03

Started making content, ended up running the conference. Three years at Portugal's largest free tech event — 5,000+ attendees, 85+ partner companies, entirely free to attend.

As Coordinator (2024–2025), led a 30-person team through the full event cycle: curating speakers, securing Tier-1 sponsorships including Oracle, and coordinating national media coverage through CNN Portugal. As Multimedia Team Member (2022–2024), handled production and communications across two consecutive editions.

04

Built the HR function from scratch for a 20+ person student team at IST's student newspaper. No existing process — recruitment, onboarding, retention — all of it written from nothing.

The goal: get the right people in, and make sure they stayed. First-year retention improved, and so did how quickly new members found their footing.

05

One edition of WebSummit in Lisbon. Guided speakers, press, and partners through a multi-day, multi-venue event where thousands of things can go wrong — and usually don't, because people behind the scenes keep it together.

06

7 years teaching two completely different things to completely different people. Maths and Physics to students who needed structured thinking. Ballet to children who needed to trust their bodies. Both came down to the same skill: breaking something hard into steps someone else can follow.

07

3 years of weekly food rescue across Lisbon — collecting surplus from restaurants and supermarkets, delivering to families in need. The kind of work that doesn't show up in a CV the way it should.

08

Portugal's national food bank campaigns — annual collection drives, warehouse sorting, and distribution across Lisbon. The kind of volunteering you start young and don't stop thinking about.
